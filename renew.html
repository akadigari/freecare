Got a Medicaid work-rule letter? Build your proof packet

Starting January 2027, Maryland checks that some adults on Medicaid work or do other approved activities. If Maryland Health Connection asks you for proof, this page helps you put one month of proof together in one PDF.

Nothing you type or photograph leaves this device. Nothing you enter is saved after you close the page.

Not sure the rules apply to you? Maryland has its own online tool and a phone line: Maryland Health Connection Medicaid Check-In, .

The rule looks at one month at a time. Start with the most recent full month.
